# AutoInsurance
Performed automatic regression methods to uncover the significant predictor variables influencing the monthly premium.
Vehicle Class, Coverage type, Total Claim Amount, Location, Customer Lifetime Value stood out to be the top predictor variables based on the statistical significance of alpha < 0.05
# Descriptions of Methods Employed
Principal Component Analysis (PCA): PCA was employed to reduce the dimensionality of the
data, simplifying complex datasets into key components. This method helps in identifying the
underlying factors that drive claim amounts and policy specifications by condensing the
information into fewer variables, while retaining most of the original data’s variability.
Correspondence Analysis: T o understand the factors affecting insurance type selection. This
method examines the relationship between Coverage Type and other factors by visualizing their
associations and highlighting patterns within the data.
Automatic Regression Methods: Multiple automatic regression techniques were applied to
determine the main indicators of the Monthly Premium. These methods allow the significant
predictors of monthly premium to be explored by modeling the relationship between the
premium amount and various explanatory variables.
Lasso Regression and OLS Regression: The study also aimed to explain the T otal Claim
Rate based on the insured customer’s background. Lasso Regression was used to identify the
most important predictors, while Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Regression provided a more
traditional approach to modeling the relationship between the claim rate and customer
characteristics. These methods together help in understanding how background factors impact
claim rates
